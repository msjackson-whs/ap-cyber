The Harbor Point Breach

An attacker crew broke into a medical supply company and left with its entire customer database. The story of how they did it has been scrambled. Put it back together.

How to complete this activity

  1. Read all six paragraphs.
  2. Drag each paragraph by its handle, or use the arrow buttons, until the story is in the order it happened.
  3. Choose the attack phase that each paragraph shows. Each phase is used exactly once.
  4. Select Check my answers. Fix anything marked and check again until everything is correct.
